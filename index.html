Reasons Within

Understand your body’s patterns.

Reasons Within reads the Apple Health data you permit—sleep, activity, recovery signals, cycle context, daylight, heart health, and more—and compares it with your own baseline. Add a private Feel check-in to connect the measurements with how you actually felt.

Your health data, check-ins, reviews, and explanations are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to Reasons Within. Optional analytics helps us improve the app and never includes health data or personal notes. See the Privacy Policy.

What it does

  • Daily context — readiness and recent changes explained against your usual range.
  • Personal patterns — findings begin as building evidence and are confirmed only when they keep holding.
  • Weekly and Monthly Reviews — calm summaries of what changed, what held, and what remains uncertain.
  • Recovery forecasts — shown after an activity day only when enough confirmed personal history exists.
  • Health and Bio Age trends — including an eight-week Bio Age trajectory with Reasons Within Plus.
  • Private history — revisit reviews and pattern changes in the on-device Plus Archive.
  • Home Screen widgets — Today, Bio Age, Activity, and Calories.

Free and Plus

Daily readiness, Feel, Health, Trends, Pattern Evidence, and your first Weekly Review remain free. Reasons Within Plus adds a new Weekly Review every week, Monthly Reviews, eligible recovery forecasts, the eight-week Bio Age trajectory, and the Analysis and Pattern Archive. Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically until canceled through your Apple Account.
